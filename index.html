How this works. Trace Lookup queries free public APIs directly from your browser and shows the data inline — profile name, photo, handle, follower counts, join dates, bio, location, breach exposure, DNS. When it finds an email, location or website in a profile, it automatically runs a follow-up search on it.
What a free page can't do. Getting a stranger's home address or phone number from their email or name needs the paid people-search services (those block direct browser access and gate data behind accounts). Those remain as "deeper search" links that open the source with your query filled in. Breach data shows which breaches an email is in and what categories leaked — never the leaked passwords themselves.
Use it responsibly. US law (FCRA) bars using people-search data for hiring, tenant screening, credit or insurance. Checking your own footprint is the best use. Don't use this to stalk, harass or endanger anyone.
